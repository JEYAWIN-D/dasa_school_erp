# 11. Missing Features & Enterprise Recommendations

## 1. Identified Next-Phase Enhancements
1. **Automated Parent SMS / WhatsApp Geofence Notifications:**
   - Integrate RFID or QR-code based child check-in when boarding/deboarding at stops.
   - Automatically trigger WhatsApp/SMS notification to parent: *"Dear Parent, your ward Sudeepa V.M has safely boarded VAN I at Perundurai Old Bus Stand at 07:22 AM."*
2. **Attendant Mobile Companion Web App:**
   - Create a simplified mobile view for attendants to tap and check off student attendance live during morning and evening runs.
3. **Automated Regulatory Document Expiry Alerts:**
   - Implement a daily cron task emailing the Transport In-Charge and Principal 30 days and 15 days before any vehicle fitness, insurance, or PUC expires.
4. **Fuel Economy & Mileage Analytics:**
   - Build automated kilometer-per-liter (km/L) tracking from fuel receipts and daily odometer logs to detect abnormal fuel consumption or vehicle maintenance requirements.
